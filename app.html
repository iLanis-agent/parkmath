Park Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page prices a parking stay with minute counting and bracket steps: how long the stay is, whether the grace covers it, what the rate card bills with its daily maximum, and the leave-by minute that keeps the price where it is. It uses no named constants. The grace, the first-hour price, the increment, its price and the daily maximum are yours to copy from the posted sign, and the times are yours to enter. It applies the card as written, so it has not seen your garage and does not know about validations, event rates, lost tickets, overnight rules or a barrier that charges differently, and it gives no verdict that parking there is wise or cheap. Every number here is your own entry.

The stay

Entry and exit in 24-hour form, same day.

The rate card

From the posted sign: grace, first hour, the started-block increment and its price, and the daily maximum. Prices in cents.

The stay truth

Stay: the minutes between entry and exit.

The grace truth

Grace: inside it nothing is billed; past it the whole stay is billed.

The fee truth

Fee: the first hour plus each started block, held by the daily maximum.

The leave-by truth

Leave-by: the minute the current price stops holding.
