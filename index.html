Park Math

What the garage will actually bill, and the minute the price steps up.

Boundary: arithmetic only Park Math counts minutes and applies a rate card you copy from the posted sign. It does not know your garage, its validations, event rates, lost-ticket rules or overnight pricing, and it gives no verdict that parking there is wise or cheap. Every number is your own entry.

The problem

A garage bill is not a price times hours. There is a grace that either covers the stay or vanishes, a first hour at one price, then every started block of minutes at another, and a daily maximum waiting to cut the total. The sign lists all of it; the barrier applies all of it; and the driver standing at the exit machine finds out the total only after the stay is over.

What it tells you

Enter the entry and exit times and the rate card from the sign. Park Math answers four truths with exact arithmetic: how long the stay is, whether the grace covers it or the whole stay is billed, what the card charges with its daily maximum, and the leave-by minute that keeps the price where it is before the next started block adds its price.

The honesty

Started blocks round up, because that is what the sign means by "each started 30 minutes". The grace does not discount a longer stay: past it, the whole stay is billed from entry. And when the daily maximum already holds, the page says so instead of inventing a saving.
